What this is

A free fan project

Our side of it

What you may do with this

  • Use the tools freely, for any campaign, without asking.
  • Copy or fork the repository and run your own version of the site.
  • Change the rules, the talents, the bestiary — it is a homebrew system, so make it yours.

The code and our own original writing are released under the MIT licence (see LICENSE in the repository). The material derived from Wizards of the Coast travels under the Fan Content Policy instead, and cannot be re-licensed by us or by anyone who forks this — which in practice means any copy of it must also stay free.

The one hard rule

This is never sold

No purchase, no subscription, no paywall, no ad revenue, no donation tier that unlocks anything, no login required to read it. The Fan Content Policy permits this project only for as long as that stays true — of every copy of it, including forks.
